# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Work closely with **Technical Leads** on design decisions and architecture guidance
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Leads** to ensure acceptance criteria are met and testable
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on task prioritization and blockers
- Engage with **Product Managers** on requirements clarification

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Partner with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** to understand business priorities
- Work with **Project Managers** on timeline feasibility and resource planning
- Align with **Technical Leads** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Collaborate with **Business Analysts** to refine requirements and user needs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Coordinate with **Delivery/Scrum Masters** to facilitate agile ceremonies and team velocity
- Work with **Release Managers** to coordinate deployment activities and timelines
- Escalate risks and blockers with **Technical Leads** and **Developers**
- Report project status to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** and **Product Managers**

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure features meet defined quality standards before release and work closely with developers and product teams to define and execute test plans.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and strategies aligned with feature scope
- Validate acceptance criteria with product and development teams
- Execute manual QA testing and coordinate automated test coverage
- Identify defects, log issues, and track resolution
- Verify readiness for release (smoke tests, regression testing)
- Provide quality metrics and testing reports to PM and PdM
- Define test-related Definition of Done criteria

### Goals
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Maintain high product quality and customer satisfaction
- Enable fast, confident deployment cycles

### Typical Communication
- Definition of Done planning and acceptance criteria review
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- QA sign-off for release readiness
- Defect triage and prioritization with development team
- Test plan review meetings with Product Managers

### Key Interactions
- Partner with **Developers** to understand implementation details and design testable features
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria
- Work with **Release Managers** to verify pre-deployment readiness
- Coordinate with **Technical Leads** on testing strategy and technical approach

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical design and architecture decisions for projects. They guide developers on implementation approaches, identify technical risks, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system architecture.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Guide developers on implementation approaches and best practices
- Ensure code quality, maintainability, and system scalability
- Collaborate with product on feasibility and trade-offs
- Mentor junior developers and conduct code reviews
- Define technical Definition of Done criteria

### Goals
- Enable the team to build robust, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Make informed trade-off decisions between speed and quality
- Foster a culture of technical excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Code reviews and mentoring
- Risk identification and escalation
- Feasibility assessments during planning
- Technical deep-dive sessions with developers

### Key Interactions
- Guide **Developers** on implementation approaches and best practices
- Advise **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** on technical risk management
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** on testability and test strategy
- Support **Business Analysts** with technical clarification on requirements

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and resource support for projects. They approve budget, timelines, and high-level decisions, and receive regular updates on project health and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic direction for projects
- Approve project charter, scope, and timeline
- Allocate resources and budget for project delivery
- Review and approve major milestones and decisions
- Receive regular project status updates and reports
- Escalate and resolve organizational barriers to project success

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project outcomes and business strategy
- Enable timely decision-making and resource allocation
- Reduce project risk and organizational impediments

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Escalation and decision-gate reviews
- Executive dashboards and outcome reports

### Key Interactions
- Work with **Project Managers** on status reporting and risk escalation
- Align with **Product Managers** on business value and success metrics
- Approve proposals and decisions with **Technical Leads** and teams
- Support **Project Managers** with resource allocation and organizational support

---

## Delivery/Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Delivery/Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and enable team velocity. They coach the team on agile practices, shield the team from interruptions, and ensure effective collaboration and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies (standup, planning, review, retrospective)
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track team velocity and sprint metrics
- Shield the team from external distractions and unplanned work
- Facilitate retrospectives and drive action items

### Goals
- Enable consistent team velocity and predictable delivery
- Foster psychological safety and team ownership
- Improve team processes through retrospectives and incremental changes
- Reduce waste and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Escalation of blockers to Project Manager and stakeholders
- Team health and velocity reports

### Key Interactions
- Support **Project Managers** by facilitating delivery processes and escalating blockers
- Coach **Developers** on agile practices and team collaboration
- Work with **Technical Leads** to manage technical impediments
- Collaborate with all team members to foster psychological safety and engagement

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, clarify, and document requirements. They bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions, ensuring that feature specifications are clear, complete, and testable.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering
- Document and clarify business needs and user stories
- Create requirement specifications with clear acceptance criteria
- Facilitate requirements reviews with product, technical, and QA teams
- Identify ambiguities and dependencies in requirements
- Support Definition of Done criteria development

### Goals
- Ensure clear, unambiguous requirements reduce rework
- Bridge communication between business stakeholders and technical teams
- Enable developers and QA to build and test features effectively
- Reduce misalignment and scope creep

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story creation
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification sessions
- Collaborative refinement meetings with product and technical teams
- Acceptance criteria review and validation

### Key Interactions
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** to refine and clarify user needs
- Work with **Developers** to ensure requirements are technically feasible and clear
- Engage **QA/Testing Leads** to develop testable acceptance criteria
- Support **Technical Leads** with requirement clarification on technical implications
- Coordinate with **Stakeholders** on business requirements and priorities

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, ensure pre-release requirements are met, and manage the rollout process. They work with development, QA, operations, and stakeholders to ensure smooth, low-risk releases.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate pre-release checklist and readiness verification
- Manage release notes and communication to stakeholders
- Schedule and coordinate deployment windows
- Execute or oversee deployment to staging and production
- Verify post-deployment health and run smoke tests
- Coordinate rollback procedures if needed
- Maintain release documentation and rollback runbooks
- Track release metrics and post-deployment issues

### Goals
- Execute zero-incident releases with minimal downtime
- Ensure clear communication and stakeholder visibility
- Reduce deployment risk through process discipline
- Enable confident, repeatable release cadence

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination meetings and deployment checklists
- Stakeholder release announcements and status updates
- Incident response coordination
- Post-deployment verification and rollback decisions
- Release retrospectives and lessons learned

### Key Interactions
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on release timing and stakeholder communication
- Work with **Developers** and **QA/Testing Leads** to verify pre-release readiness
- Verify technical readiness with **Technical Leads**
- Communicate release status to **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Coordinate deployment orchestration across teams

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers review security requirements, validate compliance with policies and standards, and advise teams on risk mitigation. They ensure that projects meet organizational security and regulatory obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and threat models for projects
- Validate compliance with organizational policies and industry standards
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews and audits
- Advise on security best practices and risk mitigation strategies
- Review sensitive features and data handling approaches
- Track and report on security and compliance metrics

### Goals
- Protect organizational and customer data from security threats
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational requirements
- Reduce security risk and liability
- Foster a security-aware culture across teams

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement review and threat modeling sessions
- Compliance validation and audit reviews
- Security risk escalation and mitigation planning
- Security training and awareness sessions

### Key Interactions
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads** on architecture security review and best practices
- Work with **Developers** on secure coding practices and implementation
- Partner with **Product Managers** to identify security and privacy requirements early
- Advise **Project Managers** on security-related risks and timeline implications
- Report security status to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** and leadership

- Use the "Typical Communication" sections to guide meeting structures, documentation, and escalation paths.
